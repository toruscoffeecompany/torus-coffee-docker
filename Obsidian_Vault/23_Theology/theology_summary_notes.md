# Theology Summary Notes

- Source: Vault local documents only; no external web research used.
- Theme: Sacred timing and vessel symbolism.
- Key points:
  1. Ritual timing matters more than ritual scale.
  2. The vessel—cup, hull, warehouse—is a sacred trust.
  3. Blessing cargo is less important than blessing the crew that moves it.
  4. Silence before launch is a universal sacred practice.
- Torus application: Embed short blessing rituals before major launches and inventory cycles.
