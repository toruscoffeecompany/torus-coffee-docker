## WRITING TIPS FOR THIS TEMPLATE:

✅ **Voice Check:**
- Read it aloud. Does it sound like YOU?
- Not corporate? Good.
- A little personality showing? Perfect.
- Warm and helpful? Yes.

✅ **Pacing Check:**
- No paragraphs longer than 4 sentences
- Headings every 300-400 words
- Images break up text visually
- Readers should be able to skim and still get it

✅ **SEO Check:**
- Primary keyword mentioned in: title, first 100 words, subheadings (naturally)
- Images have descriptive alt-text
- Internal links to related posts (2-3)
- Meta description written (shows in Google search)
- ~800+ words (good for SEO, not fluff)

✅ **CTA Check:**
- Does the CTA match the post topic?
- Is it subtle or does it feel forced?
- Would YOU click it?

✅ **Brand Check:**
- Does this feel like Torus (space/witchy/Midwest/genuine)?
- Is the tone consistent with your voice?
- Does it align with your business goals?
