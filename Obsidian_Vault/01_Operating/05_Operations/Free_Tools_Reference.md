## Automation / No-Code

| Tool | Best For | Free Tier | Notes |
|------|----------|-----------|-------|
| **Zapier** | Simple 2-step automations | 5 Zaps, 100 tasks/mo | Best UI, 9,000+ apps |
| **Make** | Complex multi-step workflows | 1,000 operations/mo | Better value if volume grows |
| **n8n** | Self-hosted automation | Free self-hosted | Needs server; advanced |
| **Activepieces** | Open-source alternative | Free cloud + self-hosted | MIT license, MCP support |
| **IFTTT** | Home/social/webhooks | Free basic | Simpler than Zapier |
| **Relay.app** | Human-in-the-loop approvals | 100 runs/mo | Good for review steps |

**Our choice:** Zapier first, Make if we outgrow it.
