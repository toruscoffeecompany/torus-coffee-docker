## Brand Guidelines for All Assets

**Colors:**
- Primary: Deep space blue (#0a0e27)
- Secondary: Cosmic purple (#2d1b69)
- Accent: Orange solar (#ff6b35)
- Highlight: Cyan orbit (#00d4ff)
- Text: White (#ffffff)

**Fonts:**
- Headlines: Orbitron (sci-fi feel)
- Body: Inter (clean, readable)

**Logo Placement:**
- Top center or bottom center
- Minimum 10% margin on all sides
- Never stretch or distort

**Image Style:**
- Product shots: White or dark background
- Lifestyle: Outdoor, natural light
- Social media: Bold, eye-catching, minimal text

**Templates:**
- See `08_Design_Brand/Social_Media_Template_Pack.md`
- All templates use brand colors and fonts
- Maintain consistent spacing and alignment
