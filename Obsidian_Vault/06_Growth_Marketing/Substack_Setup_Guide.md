# Substack Setup Guide — Torus Coffee Company

**Status:** Not yet created  
**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Free Tier:** Yes — Substack free plan is sufficient

## What You Need to Do

### 1. Create Substack Account
1. Go to https://substack.com
2. Click "Start writing" → "Create a new publication"
3. Publication name: "Torus Coffee Company" or "The Orbit Report"
4. URL: toruscoffeecompany.substack.com
5. Sign up with toruscoffeecompany@gmail.com

### 2. Configure Publication
- **Tagline:** "Freeze-dried snacks and coffee adventures from Iowa City"
- **Description:** Cosmic snacks for cosmic people. Freeze-dried candy, fruit, and coffee from Torus Coffee Company.
- **Logo:** Use existing brand logo from `08_Design_Brand/Logos/`
- **Cover image:** Create from product photos in `04_Products/`

### 3. Free Plan Features
- Unlimited subscribers
- Unlimited posts
- Free newsletter sending
- Basic analytics
- Custom domain option (paid upgrade, not needed now)

### 4. First Posts
1. **Welcome post:** "Why freeze-dried? Why cosmic?"
2. **Product spotlight:** "Neapolitan Orbit Cream Crunch — our flagship"
3. **Behind the scenes:** "How we freeze-dry candy"
4. **Coffee coming soon:** "We're working on coffee..."

### 5. Integration with Website
- Add Substack embed to website footer
- Add "Subscribe" button to website
- Link Substack posts in social media

### 6. What Miss Pink Needs From You
1. Confirm publication name
2. Confirm tagline
3. Approve first 3 post topics
4. Provide logo/brand assets
