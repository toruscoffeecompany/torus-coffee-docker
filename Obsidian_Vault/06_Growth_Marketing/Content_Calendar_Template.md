# Content Calendar Template — Monthly Recurring
**Brand:** Torus Coffee Company  
**Owner:** Miss Pink  
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-04  
**Purpose:** Reusable monthly schedule for Facebook, X/Twitter, and Instagram.

---

## Monthly Structure

| Week | Theme | Objective |
|------|-------|-----------|
| Week 1 | Brand Foundations | Re-introduce the brand, share values, highlight hero products. |
| Week 2 | Product Deep Dive | Spotlight 2–3 products with recipes, pairings, and comparisons. |
| Week 3 | Community & Markets | Drive foot traffic, share UGC, post live from markets. |
| Week 4 | Closing Strong | Recap month, tease next month, urgency posts (limited batches). |

---

## Weekly Post Cadence

- **Facebook:** 4–5 posts / week (market updates, carousels, longer captions).
- **X / Twitter:** 3–4 posts / week (polls, quick tips, real-time updates).
- **Instagram:** 4–5 posts / week + daily Stories (Reels, flat lays, carousels).

**Total monthly posts:** ~36–45 cross-platform posts.

---

## Weekly Template

### Monday — Motivation / Kickoff
- **All platforms:** Quote or mindset post tied to the week’s theme.
- Example: “New week, new flavor. This week we’re talking crunch.”

### Tuesday — Educational / How-To
- **Facebook:** Long-form educational post or carousel.
- **X:** Thread or single tip.
- **IG:** Reel or carousel tutorial.

### Wednesday — Product Spotlight
- **Facebook + IG:** Carousel / flat lay of the featured product.
- **X:** Single image + hook question.

### Thursday — Engagement / Community
- **All platforms:** Poll, Q&A, or UGC feature.
- Example: “Which flavor are you picking up this weekend?”

### Friday — Recipe / Pairing
- **IG:** Recipe Reel or carousel.
- **Facebook:** Full recipe in caption.
- **X:** Short pairing tip.

### Saturday — Market / Event
- **Facebook + X:** Live market post with time/location.
- **IG:** Story + post from the market.

### Sunday — Rest / Recap
- **Facebook + IG:** Weekly top sellers or customer shoutout.
- **X:** Light engagement tweet or rest.

---

## Monthly Checklist

### Content Prep
- [ ] Confirm market schedule for the month.
- [ ] Review inventory for limited-batch items.
- [ ] Gather new product photos or schedule a photoshoot.
- [ ] Collect UGC from the prior month.

### Publishing
- [ ] Schedule Week 1 posts by the 20th of the prior month.
- [ ] Schedule Week 2 posts by the 27th of the prior month.
- [ ] Schedule Week 3 posts by the 3rd of the month.
- [ ] Schedule Week 4 posts by the 10th of the month.
- [ ] Leave room for real-time market posts (schedule as placeholders).

### Analytics Review (first week of next month)
- [ ] Pull follower counts for Facebook, X, IG.
- [ ] Review top 3 posts by engagement.
- [ ] Identify lowest-performing content type.
- [ ] Adjust next month’s mix based on data.

## Notes
- Keep captions under 125 characters for X; Facebook and IG allow longer form.
- Use Instagram Stories for polls, Q&As, and “tap to shop” links when available.
- Cross-post content but rewrite captions for each platform — don’t copy-paste identical text.
- Maintain a consistent visual style: bright, clean, product-forward.
