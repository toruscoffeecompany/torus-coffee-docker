## 1. Upgrade Policy

## 4. Tool-by-Tool Upgrade Path

### Automation
- **Zapier:** Free → Starter $19.99/mo → Professional $49/mo
- **Formspree:** Free → Gold $9/mo → Platinum $34/mo
- **GitHub Actions:** Free public minutes; self-host runners if private repo limits hit

### CRM / Sales
- **HubSpot:** Free → Starter $15/mo → Professional $45/mo → Enterprise $120/mo
- **Trello:** Free Standard → Business Class $10/user/mo

### Finance
- **Wave:** Free → Plus $25/mo → Xero/QuickBooks later
- **US Bank:** CSV export stays free; paid API only if/when offered for business banking

### Marketing / Social
- **Buffer:** Free 3 channels → Essentials $6/channel/mo
- **Canva:** Free → Pro $12.99/mo
- **Mailchimp:** Free 500 contacts → Growth $10/mo

### Website / Hosting
- **Netlify:** Free → Pro $19/mo
- **Vercel:** Hobby Free → Pro $20/mo
- **Domain/hosting:** $10–20/year

### AI / Graphics
- **Local GPU:** one-time hardware, no subscription
- **Cloud render/API:** only if Sir Azure route remains blocked long-term

---

## 5. Max-Cost Scenario

If every upgrade above happens at once, the upper bound is roughly:
- **Monthly recurring:** ~$879/mo
- **Annual non-recurring:** ~$1,200–1,800
- **Per-event:** ~$100–400

This scenario assumes rapid growth. The policy is to upgrade **only as needed**, never preemptively.
