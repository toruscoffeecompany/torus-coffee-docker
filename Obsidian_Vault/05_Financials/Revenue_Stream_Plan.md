# Torus Coffee Company — Revenue Stream Plan

**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Owner:** Miss Pink  
**Status:** Active — free tier first, paid upgrades only after revenue  
**Rule:** Stay on free tools until at least one stream generates money.

## Core Principle

Torus Coffee Company will generate revenue through multiple streams.
All systems use free-tier tools until a stream proves revenue.
Only then may we upgrade tools or add paid services.

## Revenue Streams

### 1. Flea Markets & Pop-Ups
- **What:** In-person sales of freeze-dried snacks at local markets.
- **Where:** Iowa City Farmers Market, Cedar Rapids Farmers Market, regional flea markets.
- **Tools:** Square free card reader, cash, Venmo personal.
- **Cost:** $0–$50/event for table fees.
- **Revenue potential:** $200–$800/day at busy markets.
- **Status:** Vendor applications drafted, awaiting review.

### 2. Wholesale Accounts
- **What:** Bulk freeze-dried snack orders to coffee shops, co-ops, gift stores.
- **Where:** Iowa City area retailers, online wholesale inquiries.
- **Tools:** Square wholesale links, email invoices, HubSpot CRM.
- **Cost:** $0–$100 for samples/shipping.
- **Revenue potential:** $500–$2,000/month per account.
- **Status:** Supplier agreement template created, outreach pending.

### 3. Website Orders
- **What:** Direct-to-consumer orders from public website.
- **Where:** toruscoffeecompany.com (public site).
- **Tools:** Square Payment Links, free Shopify starter, or WooCommerce.
- **Cost:** $0–$29/month platform fee, 2.9% + 30¢ per transaction.
- **Revenue potential:** $300–$1,500/month at launch.
- **Status:** Website scaffold created, payment integration pending.

### 4. Amazon FBA / FBM
- **What:** Amazon listings for freeze-dried snacks.
- **Where:** Amazon marketplace.
- **Tools:** Amazon Seller Central free account, GTIN exemption ($0).
- **Cost:** $0–$39.99/month referral fee, 15% fee per sale.
- **Revenue potential:** $500–$5,000/month.
- **Status:** GTIN exemption guide created, listing setup pending.

### 5. YouTube Ad Revenue
- **What:** Torus Coffee Company channel with behind-the-scenes, recipes, market footage.
- **Where:** YouTube @TorusCoffeeCompany.
- **Tools:** YouTube Studio, free video editing (DaVinci Resolve, CapCut).
- **Cost:** $0.
- **Revenue potential:** $100–$1,000/month at 1K–10K views/day.
- **Status:** Channel exists, content plan created.

### 6. Sponsorships & Brand Deals
- **What:** Paid partnerships with equipment brands, snack brands, local businesses.
- **Where:** Instagram, YouTube, TikTok.
- **Tools:** Buffer for outreach scheduling, HubSpot CRM for contact tracking.
- **Cost:** $0.
- **Revenue potential:** $100–$5,000 per deal.
- **Status:** No deals yet, pitch deck pending.

### 7. Subscription / Club
- **What:** Monthly snack subscription box or coffee club.
- **Where:** Website, farmers markets.
- **Tools:** Square subscriptions, Cratejoy free tier, or Patreon.
- **Cost:** $0–$10/month platform fee.
- **Revenue potential:** $300–$1,500/month recurring.
- **Status:** Concept documented, not launched.

### 8. Local Delivery
- **What:** Same-day delivery within Iowa City for snacks/coffee.
- **Where:** Iowa City zip codes.
- **Tools:** DoorDash Drive free tier, personal delivery, Venmo.
- **Cost:** $0–$50/month for delivery platform fees.
- **Revenue potential:** $200–$600/month.
- **Status:** Concept documented, not launched.
