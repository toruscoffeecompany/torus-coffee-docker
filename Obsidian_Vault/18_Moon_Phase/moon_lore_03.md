# Moon Lore Note 3 — Quarter Moon Watch

- Lore: First quarter signals outward expansion; third quarter signals inward defense.
- Ritual timing:
  - First quarter: send couriers, launch promotions, inspect outbound cargo.
  - Third quarter: repair hulls, audit defenses, prune crew access logs.
- Torus tie-in: Mirror quarter moon timing for ops sprints vs maintenance days.
