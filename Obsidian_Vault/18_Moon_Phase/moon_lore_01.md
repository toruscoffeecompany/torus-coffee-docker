# Moon Lore Note 1 — Full Moon Navigation

- Lore: Full moon light was used by early sailors and coastal traders to gauge tide strength and fish movement.
- Ritual timing: Best for finalizing voyage plans, loading cargo under high tide, and holding captain’s councils.
- Torus tie-in: Brewbeard Ledgerbane preferred to weigh anchor during waning full moon for calmer seas and clearer accounting.
