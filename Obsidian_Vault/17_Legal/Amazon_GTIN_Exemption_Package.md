**Cost:** $0

## Overview

Amazon requires GTINs (Global Trade Item Numbers) for most products. However, Torus Coffee Company qualifies for a GTIN exemption because:
- We are a small business/new brand
- Our products are private label/freeze-dried
- We don't have UPCs/EANs assigned yet

## Exemption Types

### 1. GTIN Exemption for Brands
- **Eligibility:** New brands without existing GTINs
- **Process:** Apply through Amazon Brand Registry
- **Timeline:** 2-4 weeks for approval

### 2. GTIN Exemption for Products
- **Eligibility:** Individual products without GTINs
- **Process:** Apply through Seller Central
- **Timeline:** 1-2 weeks for approval

## Application Steps

### Step 1: Prepare Documentation
- [x] Business registration documents
- [x] Product photos with labels
- [x] Product descriptions
- [x] Brand documentation
- [ ] Application letter

### Step 2: Submit Application
1. Log into Amazon Seller Central
2. Go to Inventory → Add Products → GTIN Exemption
3. Select exemption type
4. Upload required documents
5. Submit application

### Step 3: Follow Up
- Monitor email for status updates
- Respond to any Amazon requests within 5 business days
- Approval typically takes 1-2 weeks

## Required Documents

### For Brand Exemption
1. **Business License** — LLC registration
2. **EIN Confirmation** — IRS EIN letter
3. **Brand Documentation** — logo, brand guidelines
4. **Product Images** — clear photos with labels
5. **Product List** — all SKUs requesting exemption

### For Product Exemption
1. **Product Title** — exact Amazon listing title
2. **Brand** — Torus Coffee Company
3. **Product Type** — Food/Snacks
4. **Product Images** — main image + alternate views
5. **Product Description** — bullet points + description
