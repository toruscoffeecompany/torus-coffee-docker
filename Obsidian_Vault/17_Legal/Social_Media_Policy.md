# Torus Coffee Company — Social Media Policy

## Purpose
This policy establishes guidelines for all Torus Coffee Company social media activity. It applies to owners, staff, contractors, and anyone posting on behalf of the brand.

## Brand Voice
- **Tone:** Warm, pirate-grit, coffee-first
- **Accent color:** Coral-pink #FF6F91
- **Style:** Approachable, knowledgeable, slightly irreverent
- **Do:** Use real language. Avoid corporate jargon.
- **Don't:** Oversell. Be honest about product and process.

## Platform Rules

### Instagram / Facebook
- Post frequency: 3–5x/week minimum
- Content mix: 40% product, 30% behind-the-scenes, 20% community, 10% educational
- Tag policy: Tag customers only with explicit permission
- Hashtags: Use brand hashtags + 3–5 relevant tags per post
- Stories: Daily during events, 2–3x/week otherwise

### TikTok / YouTube Shorts
- Video length: 15–60 seconds
- Focus: Process, people, product education
- Music: Royalty-free or licensed only
- Captions: Always include for accessibility

### Pinterest
- Pin format: Product + lifestyle + recipe pins
- Link policy: All pins link to toruscoffeecompany.com
- Board organization: By product line, then by use case

### X / Twitter
- Post frequency: 1–2x/day
- Engagement: Respond to mentions within 4 business hours
- Threads: Use for origin stories, process deep-dives, announcements

## Content Approval
- Owners (Miss Pink / Sara Jane Shelvock): Final approval on all brand content
- Staff: May post with prior written approval
- Contractors: Must submit draft for approval before publishing

## Confidentiality
- Never disclose unreleased products, financials, or internal operations
- No sharing of customer data without consent
- No posting of staff without signed media release

## Crisis Response
- Negative comments: Respond within 2 hours, escalate to owner if unresolved
- PR issues: Pause scheduled posts, consult owner before responding publicly
- Documentation: Log all incidents in `09_Projects/Trello_Boards/Business_Docs/`

## Scheduling
- Primary scheduler: Buffer / Later (free tier)
- Backup: Manual posting if scheduler fails
- Review calendar: Weekly on Mondays

## Compliance
- FTC disclosure: Always disclose paid partnerships with #ad or #sponsored
- Age restrictions: No marketing to under-13s
- Platform TOS: Must comply with each platform's current Terms of Service

## Review Cycle
- Reviewed quarterly
- Next review: 2026-11-03
- Owner approval required for changes
