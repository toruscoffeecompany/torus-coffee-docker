## What is a GTIN?
- **GTIN** = Global Trade Item Number
- Required by most retailers (Amazon, Walmart, grocery stores)
- Format for small packages: **UPC-A** (12 digits) or **GTIN-14**
- Amazon accepts GTIN exemption for handmade/manufactured products

## Cost Estimate

### Option A: GS1 US Membership (Recommended for Retail)
| Plan | Annual Cost | Includes | Best For |
|------|-------------|----------|----------|
| GS1 US Membership | $250-$500/year | 1 company prefix + 10-100 product prefixes | Small business starting retail |
| Per additional prefix | $50-$100 | Extra product lines | Expanding catalog |

**Total for 10 products:** ~$250-$500/year

### Option B: Amazon GTIN Exemption (Free)
- Amazon allows GTIN exemption for:
  - Handmade/manufactured products
  - Products without existing GTINs
  - Brands applying to Amazon Brand Registry
- **Cost:** $0
- **Limitation:** Only works for Amazon, not grocery stores

### Option C: Buy UPCs from Resellers (Not Recommended)
- Cost: $5-$25 per UPC
- **Risk:** Many retailers reject reseller UPCs
- **Not recommended** for long-term retail

## Recommended Path for Torus Coffee

**Phase 1 (Now):** Apply for Amazon GTIN exemption
- Cost: $0
- Time: 1-2 weeks
- Allows listing on Amazon

**Phase 2 (When Ready for Retail):** Join GS1 US
- Cost: ~$250-$500/year
- Time: 1-2 weeks for prefix
- Allows grocery store listings

## Application Steps

### GS1 US Application
1. Go to https://www.gs1us.org/get-a-gtin
2. Create account as "Torus Coffee Company LLC"
3. Select "Small Business" plan
4. Pay annual fee
5. Receive company prefix (6-9 digits)
6. Generate GTINs for each product using GS1 US Data Hub

### Amazon GTIN Exemption
1. Go to https://brandservices.amazon.com/
2. Apply for Brand Registry
3. Request GTIN exemption for each product
4. Provide product photos + brand info
5. Wait 1-2 weeks for approval
