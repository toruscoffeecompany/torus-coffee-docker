## What is Amazon GTIN Exemption?
- Amazon allows brands to list products without GTINs if:
  - Products are handmade/manufactured by the brand
  - Products do not already have GTINs
  - Brand applies for Brand Registry exemption

## Eligibility Check
- [x] Products are manufactured by Torus Coffee Company
- [x] Products do not have existing GTINs
- [x] Brand has unique packaging/labels

5. **Website** — toruscoffeecompany.com (needs to be live)

## Application Steps
1. Create Amazon Brand Registry account
2. Apply for GTIN exemption for each product
3. Upload product photos + brand info
4. Wait 1-2 weeks for approval
5. List products in Amazon Seller Central
