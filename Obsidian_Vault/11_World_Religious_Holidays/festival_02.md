# Festival Entry 2 — Tide Market Fair

- Date: First weekend of May, when spring tides are strongest.
- Lore: Market stalls are traditionally built on stilts; honor the water that carries trade.
- Ritual timing: Open sales at high tide, close at ebb; offer a libation of coffee to the dock pilings.
- Torus tie-in: Run pop-up tastings and wholesale outreach during this fair.
