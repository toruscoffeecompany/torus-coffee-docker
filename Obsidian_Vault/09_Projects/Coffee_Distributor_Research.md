## Distributors Researched

### 1. Sweet Maria's (https://www.sweetmarias.com/)
- **Location:** Online, based in California
- **Products:** Green coffee beans, roasting equipment
- **Minimum order:** $50 minimum
- **Pricing:** $3-$8/lb for green beans
- **Shipping:** 3-5 days, $10-$15 flat rate
- **Pros:** Wide selection, educational resources, fair trade options
- **Cons:** West coast based, shipping costs
- **Contact:** sales@sweetmarias.com

### 2. Royal Coffee (https://www.royalcoffee.com/)
- **Location:** California, Maryland, Texas
- **Products:** Green coffee, wholesale
- **Minimum order:** 50-100 lbs
- **Pricing:** $3-$10/lb depending on grade
- **Shipping:** 2-4 days, bulk discounts
- **Pros:** Large selection, direct trade, organic options
- **Cons:** Higher minimums
- **Contact:** (510) 539-5711
