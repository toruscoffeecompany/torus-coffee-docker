# CRM Setup — Torus Coffee Company

**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Owner:** Miss Pink  
**Status:** Guide ready — HubSpot recommended  
**Recommendation:** HubSpot CRM (free tier)

## Recommendation

**Why HubSpot CRM:**
- Best all-in-one free CRM
- 2 users, 1,000 contacts
- 2,000 emails/month
- Live chat + forms
- 30 landing pages
- 2,000+ integrations
- Easy to use, quick setup
- Can upgrade later as business grows

**Alternatives considered:**
- Zoho CRM: 5,000 records, 3 users, worse UI
- Bitrix24: unlimited users, but clunky interface
- Freshsales: 3 users, built-in phone/email
- Attio: best UI, but only 3 users, 50,000 records

**Decision:** HubSpot CRM for best balance of features, ease of use, and growth path.

## Setup Steps

### 1. Create Account
1. Go to https://hubspot.com/products/crm
2. Click "Get started free"
3. Sign up with toruscoffeecompany@gmail.com
4. Complete profile
5. Verify email
