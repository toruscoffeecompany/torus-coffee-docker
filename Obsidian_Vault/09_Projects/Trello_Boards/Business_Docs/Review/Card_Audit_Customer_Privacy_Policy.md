# Audit Customer Privacy Policy

## Description
Review and update the customer privacy policy for the website to ensure GDPR/CCPA compliance.

## Checklist

- [ ] Review current privacy policy
- [ ] Audit data collection practices (forms, cookies, analytics)
- [ ] Draft updated policy language
- [ ] Legal review
- [ ] Publish to website
- [ ] Notify customers of changes if required
