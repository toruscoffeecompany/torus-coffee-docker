# Review Insurance Coverage Policy

## Description
Annual review of general liability, workers comp, and product liability insurance.

## Checklist

- [ ] Gather current policy documents
- [ ] Compare coverage to current operations
- [ ] Identify gaps (new products, new locations)
- [ ] Request updated quotes from broker
- [ ] Update policy if needed
- [ ] Archive old policy

## Notes
Due with policy renewal in August.
