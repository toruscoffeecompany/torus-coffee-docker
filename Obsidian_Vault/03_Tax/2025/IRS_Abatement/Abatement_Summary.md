## Situation Summary

Torus Coffee Company LLC received IRS Notice CP162A (dated July 6, 2026) assessing a $510 failure-to-file penalty on the 2025 Form 1065 partnership return ($255/partner × 2 partners × 1 month late). The notice's payment deadline (July 27, 2026) had already passed.

**However:** The 2025 Form 1065 was filed via certified mail on **March 13, 2026** — three days before the due date of March 16, 2026 (March 15 fell on a Sunday).

---

## Two Arguments for Penalty Abatement

### 1. Timely Mailing (IRC §7502)

- **Certified Mail Tracking No.:** 9589 0710 5270 2716 3052 78
- **Postmark Date:** March 13, 2026, 3:49 PM (Iowa City, IA)
- **Delivery Date:** March 20, 2026, 12:30 PM (Ogden, UT)
- **Due Date:** March 16, 2026 (March 15 = Sunday, rolled to Monday)

Under IRC §7502, the postmark date is legally the filing date. USPS tracking confirms 7-day transit gap explains why IRS system shows it as "late."

### 2. Revenue Procedure 84-35 (Backup)

- Only 2 partners (both individuals)
- Both partners timely filed personal returns reporting K-1 items
- Identical 49%/51% share (Bryon Scott Smith / Sara Jane Shelvock)
- No election into centralized audit procedures
