## CONTENT BATCHING STRATEGY (Makes life easier!)

**Batch once per month:**
- Write 2-3 posts at once (when inspiration hits)
- Take all the photos you need in one session
- Edit together
- Schedule them out

This means you write less frequently but stay on schedule consistently.

## REPURPOSING BLOG CONTENT (Get more value!)

Each blog post can become:

- 📱 **Social media posts** (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook)
- 📧 **Email newsletter content** (expand on key points)
- 📌 **Pinterest pins** (especially Workshop posts)
- 📺 **Video scripts** (blog to YouTube)
- 🗣️ **Podcast episodes** (if you expand later)
- 📓 **Lead magnets** (free PDFs, checklists, guides)

**Example:** Turn "Naming Products That Sell" into:
- Blog post
- Email 3-part series
- 5 TikToks with naming tips
- Free worksheet PDF
- Pinterest pin series
