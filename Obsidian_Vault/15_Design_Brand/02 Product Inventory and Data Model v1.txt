Torus Coffee Company - Product Inventory and Data Model Design
Last updated: 2026-05-29

Purpose
This document defines the product database, inventory model, pricing controls, product images, and product-feed fields needed for the Torus Coffee Company website.

Core Principle
Products are the heart of the website. The product database should be designed once and reused everywhere: shop pages, product pages, inventory dashboard, Square payment links, SEO, Google Merchant Center, Meta catalogs, Pinterest catalogs, email promos, and future wholesale sheets.

Product Statuses
Recommended statuses:
- Draft: Product exists in admin but not public.
- Active: Product appears on public shop pages.
- Hidden: Product does not appear publicly but data is preserved.
- Sold Out: Product appears publicly but Buy button is disabled.
- Archived: Product is retired and hidden from default admin views.

Inventory Statuses
Inventory can be calculated from count, but admin can override display status.

Recommended inventory display logic:
- Count > low stock threshold: In Stock
- Count > 0 and <= low stock threshold: Low Stock
- Count = 0: Sold Out
- Manual override: Coming Soon, Seasonal, Hidden

Do not fake scarcity.

Product Image Design
Master image principle:
Keep original product photos locally in the Website/assets area or existing Drive folders. Upload web-optimized copies for the website.

Recommended local asset structure later:
Website/assets/product-photos/product-slug/originals
Website/assets/product-photos/product-slug/web
Website/assets/product-photos/product-slug/social

Image requirements:
- Main product image should be clear and inspectable.
- Avoid dark, blurry, overly cropped images.
- Use consistent product photo style where possible.
- Create square/social-friendly versions for feeds.
- Alt text should describe the actual product.

AI image cleanup later:
Allowed use cases:
- Improve lighting/background
- Clean up distractions
- Create consistent background
- Generate lifestyle-style supporting imagery

Do not use AI images that misrepresent the actual product.

Inventory System Design

Phase 1 manual inventory:
- Admin edits quantity_on_hand manually.
- Public site shows In Stock / Low Stock / Sold Out.
- Square purchases do not automatically subtract from website inventory yet.
- Owner manually updates inventory after reviewing Square orders.

Phase 2 inventory dashboard:
- Product inventory table
- Quick +/- adjustment buttons
- Adjustment reason
- Notes
- Low-stock view
- Export inventory CSV

Future automated inventory:
- Square webhook confirms paid order.
- Server verifies Square webhook signature.
- Server maps Square item/variation to internal product.
- Inventory subtracts only after verified paid transaction.
- Adjustment is logged.

Inventory adjustment log fields:
- id
- product_id
- previous_quantity
- new_quantity
- delta
- reason: manual_adjustment, square_order, restock, correction, test, damaged, other
- note
- actor_user_id
- source: admin, square_webhook, import
- created_at

Pricing System Design

Phase 1:
- Admin/manual data controls public prices.
- Square payment link price must match website price.
- Add admin warning/checklist: after changing website price, update Square payment link/item price too.

Future:
- Square Catalog API sync or single source of truth decision.
- Price change log.
- Sale pricing.
- Bundle pricing.
- Wholesale pricing hidden behind approved wholesale account, later.

Price change log fields:
- id
- product_id
- previous_price_cents
- new_price_cents
- actor_user_id
- reason/note
- created_at

Public Inventory Display Decision
Recommended:
- Do not show exact inventory counts by default.
- Show In Stock, Low Stock, or Sold Out.
- Exact counts can be admin-only.

Exception:
For test periods, exact inventory could be shown internally or on staging only.

Test Products
Use additional created products as admin/inventory test records.

Test product rules:
- Must be Draft or Hidden unless genuinely for sale.
- Must not appear in product feeds.
- Must not have live Square buy links unless intentional.
- Use a clear tag like TEST or INTERNAL.

Data Ownership
The product database should be exportable.

Required exports later:
- Products CSV
- Inventory CSV
- Product images backup list
- Product feed CSVs
- Price history export, later

Open Decisions
1. Exact product category names.
2. Whether exact stock counts ever show publicly.
3. Whether product ingredients/allergens are ready for all products.
4. Whether Google/Meta/Pinterest feed fields are filled at launch or phased in.
5. Whether Square or Supabase is the long-term source of truth for product price/inventory.

Recommended Decision
Use Supabase as the Torus-owned product/content database and Square as checkout/payment source at first. Later, integrate Square APIs carefully and decide which system owns which data.
