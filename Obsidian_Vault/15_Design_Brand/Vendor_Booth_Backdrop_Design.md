## Production Notes

### File Requirements
- Format: PNG or PDF
- Resolution: 300 DPI minimum
- Size: 3600x2880 pixels (10x8 feet at 300 DPI)
- Color mode: RGB

### Printing
- Vendor: Local print shop or online (Vistaprint, BannerBuzz)
- Material: Vinyl banner (13oz scrim) or fabric
- Finish: Matte or gloss
- Grommets: Every 2 feet for hanging

### Alternate Versions
1. **Small tabletop:** 4x2 feet for card tables
2. **Medium booth:** 8x6 feet for standard booths
3. **Large booth:** 10x8 feet for prime locations
