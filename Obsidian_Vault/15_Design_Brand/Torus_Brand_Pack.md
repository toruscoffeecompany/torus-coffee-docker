- **Blog / newsletter:** `08_Design_Brand/The Orbit Report Blog Logo v1.png`

### Usage Rules
- **Min clear space:** 1 × logo height on all sides.
- **Min width:** 200 px for print; 120 px for digital.
- **Do not:** stretch, recolor, add shadows/glows, or place on busy backgrounds without a solid contrast pass.
- **Dark backgrounds:** use white or light-cyan logo variant.
- **Small icons / avatars:** use simplified mark only (no tagline).

### File Naming
```
Torus_Coffee_Logo_{Context}_{Variant}.{ext}
```
Examples:
- `Torus_Coffee_Logo_Light_v3.jpg`
- `Torus_Coffee_Logo_Watermark_v2.png`

### Body & UI
| Role | Fonts | Fallback | Usage |
|------|-------|----------|-------|
| **Body text** | Inter | sans-serif | Descriptions, policies, forms, long-form |
| **Body alt** | Roboto, Open Sans | sans-serif | Web body, emails, documents |

### Scale
- **H1:** 2.5 rem / 40 px (Exo 2 Bold)
- **H2:** 2 rem / 32 px (Exo 2 SemiBold)
- **H3:** 1.5 rem / 24 px (Rajdhani Bold)
- **Body:** 1 rem / 16 px (Inter Regular)
- **Small:** 0.875 rem / 14 px (Inter Regular)
- **Caption:** 0.75 rem / 12 px (Inter Medium)

## 4. Voice & Tone

### Brand Personality
- **Playful but professional** — we take quality seriously, but we don't take ourselves too seriously.
- **Cosmic metaphors** — space language is native, not forced.
- **Family-friendly** — no edge, no adult humor.
- **Local pride** — Iowa City roots matter.
- **Educational when needed** — explain freeze-drying, sourcing, or process without being preachy.

### Vocabulary
| Use | Avoid |
|-----|-------|
| Cosmic, orbit, galaxy, nebula, stardust | Corporate jargon, "synergy" |
| Crunch, freeze-dried magic, interstellar | Coffee-only language (we're a snack brand too) |
| Blast off, launch, mission | Negative framing ("problem," "pain") |
| Handcrafted in Iowa City | Over-promising health claims |

### Example Phrases
- "Blast off into flavor."
- "Cosmic snacks for cosmic people."
- "Taste the galaxy."
- "Freeze-dried magic from Iowa City."
- "Stay curious. Stay crunchy. Stay cosmic."

### Sentence Rhythm
- Short punchy lines for packaging and social.
- 1–2 sentence product descriptions.
- 3–5 bullet benefits max.
- Em dashes for parenthetical energy.

## 5. Photography & Visual Style

### Product Photography
- **Background:** white for e-commerce; cosmic gradient (Deep Space Purple → Starlight Blue) for brand shots.
- **Lighting:** bright, even, minimal harsh shadows.
- **Composition:** product centered or ⅔ rule; show texture (crunch crystals, freeze-dried structure).
- **Props:** minimal — star stickers, constellation cards, rustic wood, or clear acrylic stands.
- **Style:** authentic, not overly polished. Slight vignette OK for social.

### Lifestyle & Action
- **Subjects:** real people, diverse ages, outdoor/adventure context (camping, stargazing, farmers markets).
- **Mood:** warm, energetic, curious.
- **Framing:** medium shots for hands holding bags; close-ups for eating moments.
- **Behind-the-scenes:** freeze-dryer batches, packing parties, Miss Pink at market stall.

### Social Media Guidelines
- **Filters:** consistent warm-cool split (cosmic blue shadows, warm highlights).
- **Text overlays:** bold, legible, 1–2 lines max.
- **Aspect ratios:** 1:1 for feed, 9:16 for Reels/TikTok, 16:9 for YouTube.
- **B-roll:** slow-pour cold brew, freeze-dried crunch close-up, bag opening with product spilling out.

## 6. Packaging Templates

### Bag Specs
| Size | Dimensions (in) | Weight Range | Use |
|------|-----------------|--------------|-----|
| **Small** | 4 × 6 | 0.5 oz – 1.15 oz | Solar Strawberries, Apple Cinnamon Comets, Apple Zephyr Chips, Star-Dusted Banana Crunch |
| **Medium** | 5 × 7 | 1.55 oz – 2.0 oz | Cosmic Bananas, Neapolitan Orbit Cream Crunch, Orbit Cream Crunch |
| **Large** | 6 × 8 | 2.6 oz | Aurora Berryalis, Sour Aurora Bites, Aurora Bites |

### Front Label Template
```
┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│ [LOGO / MARK]        [RIBBON]  │
│                                 │
│   PRODUCT NAME                  │
│   Cosmic Subtitle               │
│                                 │
│   [PRODUCT PHOTO]               │
│                                 │
│   $PRICE      NET WT X.X OZ     │
└─────────────────────────────────┘
```
- **Logo:** top-left, 20% of label height.
- **Ribbon:** top-right if applicable ("New Arrival", "Limited").
- **Product name:** bold, Exo 2, 14 pt minimum.
- **Price:** right-aligned, Cosmic Indigo, 12 pt.
- **Weight:** bottom-right, 9 pt.
- **QR code:** bottom-left, links to product page or nutrition info.

### Back Label Template
```
┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│ [NUTRITION FACLES TABLE]        │
│                                 │
│ Ingredients: ...                 │
│                                 │
│ Distributed by Torus Coffee      │
│ Company LLC, Iowa City, IA      │
│                                 │
│ [GTIN / UPC WHEN READY]         │
│ [QR CODE → traceability]        │
└─────────────────────────────────┘
```
- **Nutrition facts:** FDA-compliant layout, 8 pt minimum.
- **Ingredients:** bold allergens, regular text for rest.
- **Distributor:** legal name + city/state.
- **Barcode:** bottom-right, 1" min height.
- **QR:** bottom-left, 0.75" min.

### Color Printing Notes
- **Bag material:** clear matte or crystal-clear poly.
- **Inks:** CMYK + 1 spot (Cosmic Indigo or Nebula Pink for brand reinforcement).
- **Safe zone:** 0.125" bleed on all sides; keep critical text 0.25" from trim.
- **File format:** PDF/X-1a with 300 dpi raster, all fonts outlined.

### Seasonal / Limited-Edition Overlays
- Halloween: deep orange + black neon green.
- Winter: ice-blue gradient + silver foil.
- Spring: Aurora Pink + Emerald Nebula pastels.
