## Part 3: Blog Post Template (Ready to Use!)

### Header Section
```
---
Title: [Post Title - Should Be Catchy & Clear]
Date: [Publish Date]
Author: [Sara Jane or Husband Name]
Category: [Behind the Cosmos / Orbit Workshop / The Orbit Report]
Reading Time: [Auto-calculate: ~250 words per minute]
Featured Image: [High-quality image that represents the post]
---
```

### Opening Hook (150 words)
Start with a story, question, or relatable moment. Make them WANT to read.
Example: "I burned three batches of freeze-dried strawberries before I understood humidity levels..."

### Body Sections (Use H2 & H3 Headings)
- Break into 3-5 digestible sections
- Use bolding for key takeaways
- Include 1-2 images per section
- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max for readability)

### Practical Takeaway (If Workshop/Pillar 2)
A numbered list, checklist, or step-by-step guide readers can actually use.

### Personal Connection (If Behind the Cosmos/Pillar 1)
Tie it back to your values, your why, your vision for Torus.

### Call-to-Action (Subtle, Not Salesy)
Choose ONE per post:
- "Browse our [Product Category]"
- "Sign up for bi-weekly business tips"
- "Share your own freeze-dried story in the comments"
- "Download our free [Worksheet/Checklist]"
- "Follow us on TikTok for quick tips"

### Footer
- Author bio (1-2 sentences)
- Tags (3-5 relevant keywords)
- Related posts (link to 2-3 previous posts)
- Social share buttons

## Part 4: Tone & Voice Guide

**Your Brand Voice:** Friendly, knowledgeable, a little witchy, deeply Midwest, genuinely helpful

### DO:
✅ Be honest about failures and lessons learned  
✅ Use conversational language ("we learned..." not "one should consider...")  
✅ Sprinkle in Midwest charm and humor  
✅ Show personality and values  
✅ Give away real information (build trust)  
✅ Use emojis sparingly but purposefully (fits the vibe)  
✅ Tell stories—data + narrative is more powerful  

### DON'T:
❌ Sound like a corporation (you're not)  
❌ Over-sell your products (readers can smell it)  
❌ Use jargon without explaining it  
❌ Ignore comments or community feedback  
❌ Post fake content or exaggerate numbers  
❌ Sound preachy or condescending  
❌ Forget your Midwest roots (stay authentic)  

### Example Voice Comparison

**❌ Corporate (No):**
"Our premium freeze-dried offerings utilize advanced moisture-extraction technology to provide optimal snacking convenience."

**✅ Torus Voice (Yes):**
"We freeze-dry our snacks until they're perfectly crunchy—basically, all the flavor without the moisture. It's low-key magic."

## Part 5: SEO Tips for Your Blog

**Goal:** Get found by people searching "how to start freeze-dried business" or "freeze-dried snack recipes"

### Before You Publish:

1. **Pick a Primary Keyword** (what do you want this post ranked for?)
   - Example: "how to freeze-dry candy at home"
   - Use free tools: Google Keyword Planner, Ubersuggest, Answer the Public

2. **Title Should Include the Keyword**
   - ✅ "How to Freeze-Dry Candy at Home: Step-by-Step Guide"
   - ❌ "Making Magic: Our Candy Process"

3. **First 100 words should mention keyword naturally** (2-3 times max)

4. **Use subheadings (H2, H3)** with keywords where they fit naturally
   - Helps readers scan + helps Google understand structure

5. **Internal Links** (link to your other blog posts)
   - Keeps readers on your site longer = better SEO

6. **Meta Description** (the snippet that shows in Google search results)
   - Keep it 150-160 characters
   - Include keyword naturally
   - Example: "Learn how to freeze-dry candy at home with our complete beginner's guide. Includes equipment recommendations and cost breakdown."

7. **Image Alt Text** (describe images for accessibility + SEO)
   - ✅ "Sara Jane holding freeze-dryer with strawberry candies"
   - ❌ "image1.jpg"

8. **Aim for 800+ words** (longer posts rank better, but only if content is good)

## Part 7: Images & Visual Strategy

**Where to Get Images:**
- 📱 Your phone (authenticity > perfection)
- 📷 Your own product photos
- 🎨 Canva (free, fits your brand aesthetic)
- 📸 Free stock: Unsplash, Pexels (if you need generic shots)

**Image Strategy:**
- **Hero image (1 per post):** Large, eye-catching, represents the main idea
- **In-post images (3-5):** Process shots, product shots, you at work
- **Consistency:** Use similar filters, lighting, composition across posts

**Quick Design Rule:**
All images should feel "Torus" — space/witchy vibes, warm lighting, authentic (not overly styled)

## Part 8: Call-to-Action Strategy (The Subtle Sell)

You have 3 types of CTAs. Use them strategically:

### Type 1: Direct Product CTA
*Use in: Orbit Workshop, stories about the product, seasonal posts*
- "Shop our Strawberry Cosmos (these freeze beautifully!)"
- "Check out our New! Midnight Indigo Collection"
- "Browse freeze-dried candies that inspired this post"

### Type 2: Email Signup CTA
*Use in: Posts with deep educational value*
- "Get bi-weekly business tips + exclusive sneak peeks"
- "Join 500+ small biz owners getting freeze-dry guides in their inbox"

### Type 3: Community/Engagement CTA
*Use in: Pillar 3 stories, relatable posts*
- "What's your favorite freeze-dried snack combo? Tell us in the comments!"
- "Share your own small-biz journey in the comments—we read every one"
- "Follow us on TikTok for weekly quick tips"

**The Golden Rule:** Never push a CTA that doesn't fit the post naturally. Trust = sales.

## Part 9: Monthly Check-In Metrics (Simple Tracking)

Track these to see what resonates (you don't need fancy tools):

- **Page views:** Which posts get the most traffic?
- **Comments:** Which topics spark conversation?
- **Email signups:** Which posts converted readers to subscribers?
- **Social shares:** What did your audience reshare?
- **Product clicks:** Did any posts lead to purchases?

**Monthly:** Review top 2-3 posts. What made them work? Double down on that.
