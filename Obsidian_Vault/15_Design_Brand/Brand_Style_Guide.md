# Torus Coffee Company — Brand Style Guide

**Status:** Active source of truth  
**Date:** 2026-08-03

## Brand Identity

### Name
Torus Coffee Company LLC

### Tagline
"Cosmic coffee, freeze-dried candy, and celestial snacks handcrafted in Iowa City"

### Personality
- Fun, cosmic, playful
- Bold but approachable
- Space-themed but family-friendly
- Quality-focused
- Local Iowa pride

## Color Palette

### Primary Colors
- **Cosmic Purple:** #2D1B4E (deep space purple)
- **Aurora Pink:** #FF6B9D (nebula pink)
- **Starlight Blue:** #4A90E2 (deep sky blue)
- **Solar Yellow:** #FFD93D (sun yellow)
- **Orbit Orange:** #FF8C42 (comet orange)

### Secondary Colors
- **Aurora Green:** #7ED321 (cosmic green)
- **Berry Purple:** #9B59B6 (berry purple)
- **Cosmic Black:** #1A1A2E (space black)
- **Starlight White:** #FFFFFF

### Neutrals
- **Moon Gray:** #F5F5F5
- **Shadow Gray:** #666666
- **Deep Space:** #0D0D0D

## Typography

### Headlines
- **Font:** Bold, futuristic, space-themed
- **Examples:** Orbitron, Exo 2, Rajdhani
- **Use:** Product names, logos, banners

### Body Text
- **Font:** Clean, readable
- **Examples:** Inter, Roboto, Open Sans
- **Use:** Descriptions, policies, forms

### Accent Text
- **Font:** Handwritten, playful
- **Examples:** Caveat, Patrick Hand
- **Use:** Quotes, special notes, stickers

## Logo Usage

### Primary Logo
- **Light backgrounds:** `08_Design_Brand/Logos/Torus Coffee Company Logo v3.jpg`
- **Website header:** `06_Website/Website/Torus Coffee Company Logo Website Design v1.png`
- **Blog:** `08_Design_Brand/The Orbit Report Blog Logo v1.png`
- Min size: 200px wide
- Clear space: 1x logo height on all sides

### Logo Variations
- Light background: use dark logo
- Dark background: use light/white logo
- Small icons: use simplified mark

### Don'ts
- Don't stretch or distort
- Don't change colors
- Don't add effects (shadows, glows)
- Don't place on busy backgrounds without contrast

## Voice & Tone

### Brand Voice
- Playful but professional
- Cosmic metaphors
- Family-friendly
- Local pride
- Educational when needed

### Example Phrases
- "Blast off into flavor"
- "Cosmic snacks for cosmic people"
- "Taste the galaxy"
- "Freeze-dried magic from Iowa City"

### Avoid
- Too corporate
- Coffee-only language
- Complex jargon
- Negative framing

## Visual Style

### Photography
- Bright, colorful, cosmic
- Product-focused with space backgrounds
- Fun, action shots
- Authentic, not overly polished

### Graphics
- Bold colors
- Space/planet motifs
- Constellation patterns
- Gradient backgrounds
- Simple icons

### Social Media
- Consistent filters
- Bold text overlays
- Product close-ups
- Behind-the-scenes
- Customer photos

## Website Design

### Layout
- Clean, modern
- Mobile-first
- Fast loading
- Easy navigation

### Colors
- Purple/pink gradients
- Dark mode option
- Cosmic backgrounds
- High contrast for readability

### Imagery
- Product photos with white/cosmic backgrounds
- Lifestyle shots
- Infographics for products

## Packaging

### Materials
- Clear bags for visibility
- Bold labels
- Space-themed graphics
- QR codes for product info

### Labels
- Product name prominent
- Weight/size clear
- Nutritional facts
- Ingredients list
- GTIN barcode (when ready)

### Sizing
- Small bags: 0.5oz - 1.15oz
- Medium bags: 1.55oz - 2.0oz
- Large bags: 2.6oz

## Marketing Materials

### Business Cards
- Logo + tagline
- Contact info
- Social handles
- QR code to website

### Banners/Signage
- Bold product names
- Prices
- Cosmic theme
- QR codes

### Flyers
- Product showcase
- Event info
- Social handles
- Website URL
