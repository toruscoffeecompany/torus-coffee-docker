# Free Ecommerce Tools Reference

## Platforms with free / near-free entry
- WooCommerce: free plugin on WordPress; hosting needed separately
- Big Cartel: free up to 5 products; simple for makers
- Gumroad: free to start; creator-friendly
- Square Online: free forever plan; good for local + online sync
- Shift4Shop, Ecwid, OpenCart: free or open-source options
- PrestaShop: self-hosted, zero licensing

## Considerations
- Minimize fees until revenue validates a paid store
- Start with checkout links or static-page shop before full store build
