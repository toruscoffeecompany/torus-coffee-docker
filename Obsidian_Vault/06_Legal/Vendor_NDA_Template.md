## 3. Definition of Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by the Company to the Recipient, whether orally, electronically, in writing, or by any other means, that is designated as confidential or that a reasonable person in the Recipient's position would understand to be confidential, including without limitation:

- All trade secrets, recipes, formulas, blends, and formulations of coffee products
- Customer lists, vendor lists, supplier information, and business partner data
- Business plans, financial information, pricing, margins, and strategy
- Product development plans, research, designs, and roadmaps
- Marketing plans, campaigns, pricing strategies, and promotional materials
- Computer software, code, algorithms, architectures, and system designs
- Employee, contractor, and consultant information
- Any third-party confidential information that the Company is obligated to protect

**Exclusions:** Information shall not include information that:
- (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Recipient;
- (b) was lawfully known to the Recipient prior to disclosure by the Company;
- (c) is lawfully disclosed to the Recipient by a third party without restriction; or
- (d) is independently developed by the Recipient without use of or reference to the Company's Confidential Information.

---

## 4. Obligations

Recipient agrees to:
1. **Use** the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose set forth above;
2. **Not disclose** any Confidential Information to any third party;
3. **Protect** the Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information (but no less than reasonable care);
4. Limit access to employees, contractors, or affiliates who need to know for the Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less restrictive than this Agreement;
5. **Not reverse engineer**, disassemble, decompile, or attempt to derive the source code or trade secrets of any Confidential Information;
6. Not remove any confidentiality, copyright, trademark, or other proprietary notices.

## 6. Term

This Agreement shall commence on the date first set forth above and shall continue for **two (2) years** thereafter, unless terminated earlier by either Party upon thirty (30) days written notice. The confidentiality obligations set forth in Section 7 shall survive for **two (2) years** after the last date of disclosure of Confidential Information, regardless of termination.

---

## 7. Return or Destruction of Confidential Information

Upon the earlier of: (a) the Company's written request, or (b) termination of this Agreement, the Recipient shall:

1. **Return** all tangible materials containing Confidential Information (documents, USB drives, laptops, etc.); and
2. **Certify in writing** that all electronic copies, summaries, or derivatives of Confidential Information have been destroyed, except those retained solely for archival compliance as required by law.

Notwithstanding the foregoing, the Recipient may retain one archival copy solely to monitor its compliance with this Agreement, provided such copy remains subject to the confidentiality obligations hereunder.

---

## 8. No License

Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as granting any rights, by license or otherwise, to either Party under any patent, trademark, copyright, trade secret, or other intellectual property right of the other Party. No license or other right is granted to Recipient under any trade secret or other proprietary right of the Company.

---

## 9. No Obligation to Proceed

Nothing in this Agreement or the disclosure of Confidential Information shall be construed as a commitment by the Company to enter into any business relationship or transaction with the Recipient, and the Recipient acknowledges that the Company may, in its sole discretion, elect not to proceed with any such relationship or transaction.

---

## 10. Remedies

The Recipient acknowledges that any breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm to the Company for which monetary damages may be an inadequate remedy. Accordingly, the Company shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief (including specific performance) and any other equitable remedies available at law or equity, without the necessity of posting a bond or proving actual damages.

---

## 11. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Iowa, without regard to its conflict-of-law principles. Any legal action or proceeding arising under this Agreement shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in Iowa City, Iowa.

---

## 12. Amendment

This Agreement may not be amended, modified, or waived except by written instrument signed by both Parties.

---

## 13. Assignment

This Agreement and the rights and obligations hereunder are personal to the Recipient and may not be assigned or transferred without the prior written consent of the Company.

---

## 14. Miscellaneous

1. **Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors; this Agreement does not create a partnership, joint venture, or agency relationship.
2. **Severability.** If any provision is held invalid, the remainder shall remain in full force.
3. **Waiver.** No delay or failure to exercise any right constitutes a waiver of that right.
4. **Notices.** All notices shall be in writing and sent to the Company's address set forth above or such other address as may be designated in writing.
5. **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties regarding the subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions.
6. **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original.
7. **Force Majeure.** Neither Party shall be liable for delays or failures due to causes beyond reasonable control.
