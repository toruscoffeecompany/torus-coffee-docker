# Online Store Return & Refund Policy
**Effective Date:** August 19, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 19, 2026  
**Applies To:** All purchases made through torus.coffee online store

---

## 1. Return Eligibility

**30-day return window** from delivery date for all online store purchases.

**Exceptions (all sales final):**
- Gift cards — final sale unless damaged in transportation
- Subscription items — final sale (see §3)
- In-person event purchases — final sale (see §5)
- Damaged/incorrect items — exchange only within 45 days (see §2)

## 2. Damaged or Incorrect Orders

If your order arrives damaged or incorrect:
1. Email **admin@toruscoffeecompany.com** within **45 days** of delivery
2. Include: order number + photo of damage/packaging
3. We will **ship a replacement** — no return required in most cases

**We cover return shipping** for damaged or incorrect items.

## 3. Change-of-Mind Returns

Returns for any reason other than damage/incorrect:
- **Customer pays return shipping**
- **15% restocking fee** applies
- **Refund issued as store credit only**
- Items must be unused + in original packaging

Contact us before returning: admin@toruscoffeecompany.com

## 4. Refunds

**Refund method options:**
- Store credit (default for change-of-mind returns)
- Original payment method (for damaged/incorrect orders)

Processing time: 5–10 business days after we receive your return.

**Refunds will NOT be given for:**
- Buyer's remorse on gift cards
- Items reported after 30 days
- Subscription cancellations (unused portion only)

## 5. Subscription Policy

Subscriptions are final sale. If you cancel:
- **Prorated refund** for unused portion of current billing cycle
- No refund for partial months already delivered

You may exchange subscription items for store credit at any time.

## 6. Event & Pop-Up Purchases

Items purchased in person at markets/festivals:
- **All sales final**
- One-time exchange for same item with receipt only
- No refunds, no store credit

## 7. Gift Cards

Digital and physical gift cards:
- **Final sale**
- Cannot be refunded, exchanged, or reloaded
- No expiration date
