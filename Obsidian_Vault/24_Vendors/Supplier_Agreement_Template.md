# Supplier Agreement Template — Torus Coffee Company

**Date:** 2026-08-03  
**Owner:** Miss Pink  
**Status:** Draft for review  
**Purpose:** Standardize supplier relationships for freeze-dried candy/fruit ingredients and packaging

## Agreement Overview

This template establishes terms between Torus Coffee Company LLC ("Buyer") and suppliers ("Seller") for:
- Freeze-dried candy and fruit ingredients
- Packaging materials
- Labels and printing
- Equipment and supplies

## Key Terms

### 1. Products and Pricing
- **Product descriptions:** Detailed specs, SKUs, quality standards
- **Pricing:** FOB pricing, volume discounts, price change notifications
- **Minimum order quantities:** Specified per product
- **Lead times:** Standard and rush delivery options

### 2. Quality Standards
- **Specifications:** moisture content, particle size, color, flavor
- **Inspection:** Right to inspect/reject non-conforming goods
- **Certificates of Analysis:** Required for each shipment
- **Food safety:** FDA compliance, HACCP certification

### 3. Delivery and Shipping
- **FOB terms:** Shipping point or destination
- **Delivery schedule:** As specified in purchase orders
- **Late delivery:** Penalties for missed delivery dates
- **Shipping costs:** Specified per agreement

### 4. Payment Terms
- **Net 30:** Standard payment terms
- **Early payment discount:** 2% if paid within 10 days
- **Payment method:** Check, ACH, or wire transfer
- **Late payment:** 1.5% monthly interest on overdue balances

### 5. Confidentiality
- **Proprietary information:** Recipes, processes, pricing
- **Non-disclosure:** 3-year standard term
- **Return of materials:** Upon agreement termination

### 6. Term and Termination
- **Initial term:** 1 year from effective date
- **Renewal:** Automatic 1-year renewals unless 30-day notice
- **Termination for cause:** Material breach, insolvency
- **Termination for convenience:** 60-day notice required

### 7. Representations and Warranties
- **Authority:** Both parties have authority to enter agreement
- **Non-infringement:** Products don't violate third-party rights
- **Food safety:** Complies with all FDA and state regulations
- **Warranty period:** 90 days from delivery

### 8. Limitation of Liability
- **Consequential damages:** Excluded to maximum extent permitted by law
- **Cap on liability:** Limited to contract value
- **Indemnification:** Seller indemnifies Buyer for product defects

### 9. Insurance Requirements
- **General liability:** $1,000,000 minimum
- **Product liability:** $2,000,000 minimum
- **Workers compensation:** As required by state law
- **Certificates of insurance:** Provided before first delivery

### 10. Force Majeure
- **Events:** Acts of God, war, government action
- **Notice:** Written notice within 5 business days
- **Excused performance:** During force majeure period
- **Termination right:** If force majeure exceeds 30 days
