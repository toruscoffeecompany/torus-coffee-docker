# Freeze-Dried Production SOP

## Purpose
Standardize freeze-dried snack production for Torus Coffee Company LLC to ensure consistent quality, safety, and traceability.

## Scope
Applies to all freeze-dried production runs, including:
- 04_Products/Freeze-Dried Fruit/*
- Batch records
- Packaging and labeling

## Responsibilities
- Production operator: execute steps, record batch data
- Quality lead: verify weights, labels, and seal integrity
- Inventory lead: update inventory.json and Product_Catalog.md

## Materials
- Freeze-dried product lots
- Approved packaging materials
- Labels from 04_Products/*/Label files
- Torus Coffee Company Batch Record.xlsx

## Procedure
1. Pull batch record template from `04_Products/Torus Coffee Company Batch Record.xlsx`.
2. Confirm product SKU and label files exist for the item being produced.
3. Record lot number, batch size, start/end times, and operator initials.
4. Verify packaging weight against `Product_List_and_Prices.xlsx` tolerance.
5. Apply front/back labels from product folder.
6. Perform seal integrity and visual inspection.
7. Update `inventory.json`, `inventory_master.json`, and `orders.json` as applicable.
8. Attach completed batch record to vault and update Trello card with completion notes.

## Quality Checks
- Weight within ±2%
- Label alignment and readability
- Seal integrity: no voids or leaks
- Batch record complete before release

## Safety
- Follow food safety hygiene requirements.
- Report deviations immediately to vault/ops lead.
