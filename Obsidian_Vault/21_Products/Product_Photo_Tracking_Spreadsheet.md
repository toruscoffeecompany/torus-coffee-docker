| Use Case | Size | Format | DPI | Notes |
|----------|------|--------|-----|-------|

| Website thumb | 600x600 | PNG/JPG | 72 | Square crop |
| Social square | 1080x1080 | PNG/JPG | 72 | Instagram/Facebook |
| Social story | 1080x1920 | PNG/JPG | 72 | Instagram Stories |
| Vendor print | 3000x3000 | PNG/TIFF | 300 | High-res for print |
