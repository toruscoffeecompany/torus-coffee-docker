Torus Coffee Company - SEO Social Product Feeds and Revenue Design
Last updated: 2026-05-29

Purpose
This document defines how the website should support search visibility, free product listings, social commerce, analytics, email capture, product feeds, and revenue growth.

Core Revenue Principle
The website should not only be a place where people can buy. It should become a product and content engine that sends accurate product data to search, social, email, and future ad channels.

Primary Free/Low-Cost Growth Channels
- Google organic search
- Google Merchant Center free product listings
- Google Business Profile
- Pinterest product pins/catalogs
- Facebook/Instagram catalog later
- Blog content
- Social sharing
- Email list
- Local event/flea market traffic

SEO Goals
- Make product pages findable.
- Make blog posts useful and searchable.
- Make business info clear to Google and customers.
- Support rich product results where possible.
- Build long-term organic traffic without paid ads.

Technical SEO Requirements
- Clean page titles
- Meta descriptions
- Canonical URLs
- Sitemap.xml
- Robots.txt
- Product structured data
- Article/blog structured data, later
- Breadcrumbs
- Fast page load
- Mobile-friendly layout
- Image alt text
- Open Graph/social metadata
- Descriptive URLs

Product SEO Fields
Each product should have:
- SEO title
- SEO description
- Slug
- Canonical URL
- Main image alt text
- Social share image
- Product structured data fields
- Product feed fields

Product SEO title format examples:
- Aurora Bites 2.6oz | Torus Coffee Company
- Solar Strawberries 0.5oz Freeze-Dried Fruit | Torus Coffee Company

Product meta description examples:
- Aurora Bites are freeze-dried rainbow candy with a light, crunchy snap and fruity flavor. Secure checkout with Square. U.S. shipping only.

Product Structured Data
Use Google-supported product/merchant listing structured data where appropriate.

Product structured data should include when available:
- Product name
- Description
- Image
- Brand
- SKU/MPN
- Offer price
- Currency USD
- Availability
- URL
- Shipping/return policy details when feasible

Important:
Structured data must match visible product page content. Do not show one price publicly and another in structured data/feed.

Google Merchant Center Free Listings
Goal:
Get eligible products shown for free across Google surfaces.

Needed:
- Google Merchant Center account
- Claimed/verified website
- Product feed or crawled product data
- Shipping policy/settings
- Return policy
- Accurate product pages

Product feed fields to prepare:
- id
- title
- description
- link
- image_link
- additional_image_link later
- availability
- price
- condition
- brand
- mpn/gtin if applicable
- shipping, if using feed-level shipping

Design requirement:
Admin product fields should map cleanly to Google feed export.

Google Business Profile
Use for local credibility.

Potential uses:
- Show business info
- Add product highlights manually
- Collect reviews later
- Link to website/shop
- Share updates/events

Website should link to Google profile if appropriate later.

Useful later:

Email List Design
Email list is a direct revenue channel.

Signup locations:
- Home page
- Footer
- Blog posts
- Product pages, optional
- Sold-out/restock notification later

Signup promise examples:
- Restocks, launches, recipes, and cosmic snack news.
- Product drops, market dates, and The Orbit Kitchen recipes.

Consent requirements:
- Clear opt-in language
- Store timestamp/source
- Unsubscribe handled by future email platform

Email signup fields:
- email
- first_name, optional
- source page
- interests, optional later
- marketing_consent true
- created_at

Analytics
Phase 1 options:
- Google Analytics
- Plausible or privacy-friendly alternative, if desired
- Hosting analytics

Minimum metrics to track:
- Page views
- Product page views
- Buy button clicks
- Email signups
- Contact form submissions
- Blog post views
- Traffic sources

Important limitation:
With Square payment links, the website may track buy button clicks but not complete purchase conversion unless Square/analytics integration is added.

Future metrics:
- Completed orders from Square API/webhooks
- Product conversion rate
- Inventory sell-through
- Email signup to purchase
- Blog to product clicks
- Product feed performance

Phase 1:

Open Decisions
1. Which analytics tool to use at launch.
2. Whether Google Merchant Center setup happens before or after public launch.
3. Whether Pinterest catalog is included in Phase 2 or later.
4. Whether Meta Pixel is acceptable at launch from privacy/simplicity standpoint.
5. Whether product reviews/testimonials launch immediately.

Recommended Decision
Build product and SEO/feed fields from the start, launch with basic analytics and Google Search Console, then add Google Merchant Center free listings as soon as product pages, policies, and shipping settings are stable.
