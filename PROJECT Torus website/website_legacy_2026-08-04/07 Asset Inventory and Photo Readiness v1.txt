4. Product Photo Readiness Checklist
------------------------------------------------------------

For each launch product, try to collect:
- Front-of-bag/package photo
- Product close-up showing texture
- Lifestyle/table photo
- White/clean background product image, if possible
- Square crop for shop cards
- Wide crop for social/hero use, optional

Needed for each photo:
- Product name
- File location
- Permission/ownership confirmed
- Alt text
- Whether it is final or temporary

Recommended website image types:
- Main product image: clear square image
- Gallery image 1: packaging/product bag
- Gallery image 2: texture close-up
- Gallery image 3: lifestyle/market/serving photo
- Social share image: 1200x630-ish later

------------------------------------------------------------
5. Launch Recommendation
------------------------------------------------------------

Best launch approach:
- Use real product photos for product cards/pages if available.
- Use product signage as supporting visuals, not the main product image, unless real photos are not ready.
- Use flea market photos on About/Find Us pages.
- Use logo/background assets for brand system.

If real product photos are missing:
- Launch with best available temporary visuals only if product pages clearly describe the products.
- Schedule a product photo session before paid ads or product feed submissions.
