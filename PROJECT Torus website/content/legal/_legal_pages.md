## Refund and Returns Policy

**Effective Date:** August 1, 2026

### Returns

We accept returns within 30 days of purchase. Products must be:
- Unopened
- In original packaging
- In resalable condition

### Refunds

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-10 business days of receiving the returned product.

### Non-Returnable Items

- Opened products (food safety)
- Custom or personalized items
- Final sale items

### Exchange

We do not offer direct exchanges. Please return the item for a refund and place a new order.

### Damaged or Defective Products

If you receive a damaged or defective product, please contact us within 7 days with photos. We will replace or refund the item.

### Return Process

1. Contact us at toruscoffeecompany@gmail.com
2. Receive return authorization
3. Ship product to: [address]
4. Refund processed within 5-10 business days

### Refund Timeline

- Product received: Day 0
- Inspection: 1-2 business days
- Refund issued: 3-5 business days
- Bank processing: 2-3 business days
