## 1. Our Commitment
Torus Coffee Company LLC (“Torus Coffee”) stands behind the quality of our freeze-dried snacks. If you are not satisfied with your purchase, we will work with you to make it right.

This policy applies to purchases made through our website, at farmers markets, and through direct sales in Iowa.

---

## 2. Damaged or Incorrect Items
If your order arrives damaged or with the wrong product:
- **Notify us within 7 days** of delivery or pickup by emailing admin@toruscoffeecompany.com or calling (319) 519-2539.
- Include photos of the damaged packaging or incorrect item, plus your order number.
- We will issue a replacement or a full refund, at your choice.

---

## 3. Product Quality Issues
If a product is stale, has an off-flavor, or is otherwise defective:
- Contact us within 14 days of purchase with details and photos if possible.
- We may offer a replacement, store credit, or refund depending on the circumstances.

---

## 4. Change of Mind
Because our products are perishable food items:
- **Opened products** cannot be returned for a refund unless defective or damaged.
- **Unopened products** may be eligible for a refund or exchange within 14 days of purchase if accompanied by a receipt. Products must be in original, sealed packaging and in resalable condition.

---

## 5. Market and Event Purchases
Purchases made in person at farmers markets or events:
- Refunds or exchanges are handled at the point of sale if you notify us immediately.
- If you cannot return to the market, contact us by email or phone within 7 days with your purchase details.

---

## 6. Refund Exclusions
We cannot issue refunds for:
- Products purchased more than 14 days ago (unless covered by a warranty defect).
- Products that have been opened, consumed, or improperly stored after delivery.
- Shipping costs unless the return is due to our error (damaged, incorrect, or defective item).

---

## 7. How Refunds Are Processed
- Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7–10 business days.
- If the original payment method is no longer available, we may issue store credit or a check mailed to your Iowa address.
- You will receive an email confirmation when your refund is processed.

---

## 8. Store Credit and Exchanges
- Store credit never expires and can be used at our website, farmers markets, or future events.
- Exchanges for different flavors or products are subject to availability.

---

## 9. Free Shipping Threshold
Orders totaling $50 or more qualify for free shipping within the contiguous United States. If a refund reduces your order total below the free-shipping threshold, original shipping costs are non-refundable unless the refund is due to our error.

---
