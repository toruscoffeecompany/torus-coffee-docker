## 2. Information We Collect
We may collect the following types of information:

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly
- **Contact details:** name, email address, phone number, mailing address.
- **Order details:** products purchased, payment information, delivery preferences.
- **Communications:** messages, inquiries, feedback, and survey responses submitted through our contact forms or in-person.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Device and usage data:** IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, time spent on site, and referring URLs.
- **Cookies and tracking:** We use cookies and similar technologies to improve site functionality and analyze traffic. See our Cookie Policy section below.

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties
- **Social media platforms:** If you interact with us on Facebook, X/Twitter, or Instagram, we may receive limited profile information (e.g., username, profile picture).
- **Payment processors:** We do not store full credit card numbers. Payment data is handled by our third-party payment processor.

- **Order processing and fulfillment:** To process payments, ship orders, and provide customer support.
- **Communication:** To send order confirmations, shipping updates, and responses to inquiries.
- **Marketing (with consent):** To send promotional emails, event reminders, and product announcements. You may opt out at any time.
