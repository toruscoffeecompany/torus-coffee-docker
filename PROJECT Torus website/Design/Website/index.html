About Torus Coffee Company

We're a small, passionate team in Iowa City creating something truly special: freeze-dried snacks and candies that taste incredible and last forever (or until winter gets too crazy and we need them).

Our philosophy is simple: help each other save your food because we know winter in the Midwest is no joke. We combine space-age freeze-drying technology with witchy, playful energy and genuine Midwest community spirit.

From flea markets to grocery store shelves—we're building something bigger. Every product is crafted with care, designed to make you smile, and made to last.
