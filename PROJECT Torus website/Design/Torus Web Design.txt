🌌 Torus Coffee Company Website Theme Guide
✦ Theme Overview:
The Torus Coffee Company website should feel like a stroll through a cozy, enchanted space emporium—part vintage bookshop, part candy alchemy lab, part celestial café. The design should balance clean minimalism with magical whimsy, evoking a space that’s warm, curious, and a little spellbound. It must appeal to both children and adults who are drawn to nostalgic treats, playful fantasy, and cosmic vibes.

The theme is a blend of:

Celestial + Witchy + Playful

Minimalist layout with pops of color and sparkle

Friendly, welcoming, and a little mysterious—like stepping into a cosmic candy spellbook.

✦ Design Goals:
Visual clarity: Easy-to-read product info, clean layout

Brand immersion: Starry, magical accents without overwhelming the content

Mobile-first responsive design

Highlight the product experience: Fun names, unique textures, vibrant visuals

✦ Suggested Color Palette:
Color Name	Hex Code	Usage
Midnight Indigo	#2A2D43	Backgrounds, headers, footers, large sections
Stardust White	#F9F9F9	Main content background, text backgrounds
Cosmic Lavender	#B49FCC	Buttons, borders, product cards
Celestial Gold	#F6C177	Highlights, icons, hover elements
Nebula Pink	#F18BA8	Accents, taglines, badges (e.g., “New!”)

✦ Typography:
Headings: Elegant with a touch of mystery
Suggested Fonts: Playfair Display, Cormorant Garamond, or Yeseva One

Body Text: Clean and modern sans-serif for readability
Suggested Fonts: Lato, Quicksand, Inter, or Poppins

✦ Visual Elements & Accents:
Starbursts, constellations, moons, potion bottles, planetary rings

Gentle floating animations or sparkles on hover

Candies illustrated as cosmic objects (e.g., a gummy worm orbiting a star)

✦ Layout & User Experience:
Homepage: Featured product carousel, short brand story, product categories

Shop Page: Filter by category (e.g., fruity, chocolatey, sour)

Product Pages: Large product images, fun descriptions, texture details

About Page: Magical brand story, founder vibe, photos from craft shows

Contact: Simple form with playful call-to-action (e.g., “Cast a Message”)

✦ Tone & Messaging:
Friendly, enchanted, a little nerdy

Use copy that evokes wonder, e.g., “Freeze-dried spells in every bag,” “From our cauldron to your cosmos,” etc.

Product names and descriptions should feel like part of a magical universe
